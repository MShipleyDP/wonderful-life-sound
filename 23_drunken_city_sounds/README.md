# Drunken City Sounds (Pottersville)

## Script Context
- **Pottersville atmosphere** - corrupt town
- More bars, gambling, vice
- Rougher street life
- Drunken people on streets
- Contrast to peaceful Bedford Falls
- Darker, seedier ambience

## What to Look For
- City ambience with edge
- Drunken voices (slurred, loud)
- Bar sounds spilling onto street
- Rougher crowd
- Police activity
- Arguments, shouting (distant)
- Broken glass, chaos

## Search Terms
- "drunk people sounds"
- "bar district ambience"
- "rowdy street sounds"
- "nightlife city sounds"
- "rough neighborhood ambience"
- "drunk crowd"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "drunk" or "rowdy crowd"
- Urban nightlife sounds

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- City/urban section
- Nighttime street scenes

## Sound Elements to Layer
1. Drunken voices (shouting, laughing)
2. Bar music leaking out (see folder 22)
3. Glass breaking (occasional)
4. Arguments/fights (distant)
5. Stumbling footsteps
6. Police sirens (distant)
7. General urban chaos

## Atmosphere
- Unsafe feeling
- Morally compromised
- Lost souls
- Hopelessness
- Corruption visible

## What to Avoid
- Modern club sounds
- Too violent/scary
- Anything not 1940s appropriate

## Contrast with Bedford Falls
- Bedford Falls: Peaceful, wholesome, safe
- Pottersville: Rough, corrupt, dangerous
- Same location, totally different feel
