# Angelic / Heavenly Sound Effects

## Script Context
- **Opening scene** - Joseph summons angels
- Angels appear throughout
- Clarence's appearances
- "Buffalo gals" sung by angels
- Magical, otherworldly moments
- Contrast with earthly Bedford Falls

## What to Look For
- Ethereal, floating quality
- Shimmering, celestial tones
- Choir pads (sustained "ahhh" sounds)
- Crystal/glass harmonica sounds
- Harp glissandos
- Wind chimes (subtle)
- Gentle, peaceful, divine quality

## Sound Design Ideas
Layer multiple elements:
1. Sustained choir "ahhh"
2. Gentle chimes or bells
3. High, shimmering tones
4. Subtle reverb/echo
5. Harp or glass harmonica

## Instruments That Work Well
- Glass harmonica/glass bowls
- Theremin (for otherworldly quality)
- Harp glissando
- Synthesizer choir pads
- Tuned wind chimes
- Crystal singing bowls

## What to Avoid
- Harsh, loud sounds
- Anything scary or demonic
- Over-the-top "Hollywood" angel choirs
- Too much treble (can be piercing)
