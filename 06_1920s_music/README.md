# 1920s Music

## Script Context
- Graduation party scene (1920s)
- Period-appropriate background music
- Charleston dance scene
- Jazz age atmosphere
- George and Mary's youth

## What to Look For
- Authentic 1920s jazz
- Charleston music (upbeat, danceable)
- Period big band arrangements
- Scratchy/vintage sound quality can add authenticity

## Search Terms
- "1920s jazz"
- "Charleston music"
- "roaring twenties music"
- "1920s dance music"
- "vintage jazz 1920s"
- "prohibition era music"

## Recommended Sources
**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Search: "1920s" or "Charleston"
- Many royalty-free period pieces

**Free Music Archive (freemusicarchive.org):**
- Jazz section, filter by vintage/1920s

**Archive.org:**
- Public domain 1920s recordings
- Authentic period recordings

**Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com):**
- "Ragtime" and period pieces
- All royalty-free with attribution

## Popular 1920s Songs (Public Domain)
- "Charleston" by James P. Johnson
- "Ain't Misbehavin'"
- "Sweet Georgia Brown"
- "Five Foot Two, Eyes of Blue"

## Style Notes
- Upbeat, lively tempo for party scenes
- Big band sound with prominent brass
- Piano rags also appropriate
- Consider instrumental versions to avoid competing with dialogue

## File Naming Convention
- `01_charleston_dance_music.mp3`
- `02_1920s_jazz_upbeat.mp3`
- `03_roaring_twenties_party.mp3`
- etc.
