# Ice Cracking Sound Effects

## Script Context
- **SPECIFIC SCENE:** Young Harry Bailey falls through ice
- George saves Harry from drowning
- Flashback sequence, winter scene
- Dramatic, tense moment

## What to Look For
- Ice breaking/cracking sounds
- Tension building - creaking before the break
- Sharp crack followed by splashing
- Cold, brittle quality

## Search Terms
- "ice cracking"
- "ice breaking"
- "frozen lake break"
- "ice snap"
- "ice sheet crack"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "ice crack" or "ice break"
- Look for dramatic, clear cracks

**Nature Sound Libraries:**
- Many environmental sound collections include ice

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Search: "ice"

## Sound Sequence Ideas
Consider layering:
1. Creaking/groaning (tension builds)
2. Sharp crack
3. Ice breaking apart
4. Splash into water

## What to Avoid
- Ice cubes in glass (too small, wrong scale)
- Mechanical breaking sounds
- Anything too "Hollywood" explosive

## File Naming Convention
- `01_ice_crack_sharp.wav`
- `02_ice_breaking_sequence.wav`
- `03_ice_creak_and_snap.wav`
- etc.
