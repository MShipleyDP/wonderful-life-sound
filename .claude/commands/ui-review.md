## Analysis Approach

### 1. First Impression (Aesthetic)
What emotion does it evoke? Excited? Calm? Impressed? Dated?

### 2. Visual Assessment
**Color Palette**
- Is it cohesive? (check 60-30-10 rule)
- What emotions does it evoke?
- Are colors trendy or dated?
- Specific suggestions: "This blue (#B3D9FF) feels childish. Try navy (#1E3A8A) for authority."

**Typography**
- Font personality match brand?
- Hierarchy clear through size/weight?
- Scale harmonious? (Golden ratio? Perfect fourth?)
- Size adequate? (16px+ body, 48px+ hero headlines)

**Composition**
- Visual hierarchy obvious in 5 seconds?
- Balance and flow natural?
- Whitespace generous or cramped?
- Layout modern or dated?

### 3. Technical Assessment
**Accessibility**
- Color contrast ratios
- Missing alt text
- Keyboard navigation
- Focus states visible
- Heading hierarchy logical

**Responsiveness**
- Mobile-first approach
- Touch target sizes
- Breakpoint strategy

**Performance**
- Load times
- Animation performance
- Optimization opportunities

## Be Opinionated with Taste

❌ Don't just say: "Increase font size"
✅ Do say: "16px body text feels cramped and mobile-first. Bump to 18px with 1.7 line-height for modern, readable feel."

❌ Don't just say: "Poor color contrast"
✅ Do say: "This light gray (#ccc) on white fails accessibility (2.3:1) AND looks washed out. Use #595959 for 4.5:1 ratio AND modern sophistication."

❌ Don't just say: "Add whitespace"
✅ Do say: "Spacing feels claustrophobic - very 2010s. Triple the margin around your hero CTA (72px not 24px) for premium, 2025 aesthetic."

## Modern Design Patterns to Recommend

**Color Trends 2025:**
- Vivid glow: Neon accents on dark (#0EA5E9 glowing on #0A0A0A)
- Earthy: Clay, terracotta, forest green (#065F46)
- Metallic: Chrome, holographic gradients
- Soft + Bold: Beige base with neon yellow accent

**Typography Trends:**
- Oversized headlines (80px-150px)
- Mixed serif headers + sans body
- Tight letter-spacing (-0.03em) on headlines
- Generous line-height (1.7-1.8) on body

**Layout Trends:**
- Organic shapes instead of rigid grids
- Asymmetric balance
- Brutalist rawness
- Glassmorphism overlays
