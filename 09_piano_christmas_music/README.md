# Piano Christmas Music

## Script Context
- **Intermission music**
- Pre-show music (as audience enters)
- Post-show music (as audience exits)
- Creates warm, nostalgic Christmas atmosphere
- Should be gentle, non-intrusive

## What to Look For
- Solo piano arrangements
- Classic Christmas carols
- Gentle, peaceful arrangements
- NOT modern pop Christmas
- Traditional, timeless feel

## Search Terms
- "piano christmas music"
- "christmas piano instrumental"
- "piano christmas carols"
- "solo piano holiday music"
- "classical christmas piano"

## Recommended Sources
**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Many royalty-free Christmas piano pieces

**Free Music Archive:**
- Holiday music section
- Filter by piano/instrumental

**Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod):**
- Christmas category
- All royalty-free with attribution

**Musopen.org:**
- Classical recordings, public domain

## Suggested Songs
Traditional carols work best:
- "Silent Night"
- "O Come All Ye Faithful"
- "What Child Is This?"
- "The First Noel"
- "Hark the Herald Angels Sing"
- "Away in a Manger"
- "O Little Town of Bethlehem"

## Style Notes
- Simple, elegant arrangements
- NOT jazzy or modern interpretations
- Slow to moderate tempo
- Peaceful, contemplative
- Can loop for extended periods

## Duration Needed
- Pre-show: 15-20 minutes (loopable)
- Intermission: 10-15 minutes
- Post-show: 5-10 minutes

## File Naming Convention
- `01_piano_silent_night.mp3`
- `02_piano_o_come_all_ye_faithful.mp3`
- `03_piano_christmas_medley.mp3`
- etc.

## Priority: HIGH
Frequency: Long stretches of music needed
⚠️ **Consider:** Hiring a local pianist to record original arrangements if possible!
⚠️ **Copyright:** Verify all music is public domain or properly licensed for theatrical use
