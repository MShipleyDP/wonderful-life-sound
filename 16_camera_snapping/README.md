# Camera Snapping Sound Effects

## Script Context
- Photography moments
- Possibly wedding photos
- Family photos
- Newspaper photographer
- 1940s camera technology

## What to Look For
- **Vintage camera shutter** (1940s era)
- Mechanical shutter click
- NOT digital camera sounds
- Flash bulb pop (optional - bright "pop" sound)
- Film advance mechanism (optional detail)

## Search Terms
- "vintage camera shutter"
- "old camera click"
- "film camera shutter"
- "1940s camera"
- "mechanical camera shutter"
- "flash bulb pop"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "camera shutter" or "film camera"
- Look for mechanical, vintage sounds

**Zapsplat:**
- Camera section
- Vintage camera category

## Camera Types from Era
- Large format cameras (bigger, heavier shutter)
- Medium format
- 35mm film cameras
- Press cameras (newspaper photographers)

## Sound Characteristics
- Mechanical "ka-chunk" sound
- Slower, heavier than modern cameras
- Distinct mirror/shutter movement
- Optional: flash bulb "POP"

## What to Avoid
- Digital camera beeps
- Modern DSLR rapid-fire shutters
- Electronic sounds
- Anything too quick/light

## File Naming Convention
- `01_vintage_camera_shutter.wav`
- `02_1940s_camera_click.wav`
- `03_camera_with_flash.wav`
- `04_press_camera_snap.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: LOW-MEDIUM
Frequency: 1-2 times, brief moments
