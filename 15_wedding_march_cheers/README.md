# Wedding March Music / Cheers

## Script Context
- George and Mary's impromptu wedding
- Romantic, joyful moment despite financial crisis
- Traditional wedding ceremony music
- Crowd cheering for newlyweds

## What to Look For
**Wedding March Music:**
- "Bridal Chorus" (Wagner's "Here Comes the Bride")
- Traditional organ or orchestral arrangement
- Can be shortened version
- Classic, timeless feel

**Crowd Cheers:**
- Small group cheering
- Warm, congratulatory
- "Hooray!" "Congratulations!" etc.
- Happy, supportive crowd

## Search Terms
**Music:**
- "wedding march"
- "bridal chorus wagner"
- "here comes the bride"
- "wedding ceremony music"

**Cheers:**
- "wedding cheers"
- "crowd applause celebration"
- "congratulations cheering"

## Recommended Sources
**Music:**
- **Musopen.org** - Classical music, public domain
- **IMSLP** - International Music Score Library (public domain)
- **YouTube Audio Library** - Wedding music

**Sound Effects:**
- **Freesound.org** - Crowd cheers
- **BBC Sound Effects** - Applause and cheers

## Musical Options
1. Traditional organ version
2. Small orchestra

- Music: 30-60 seconds (can fade)
- Cheers: 5-10 seconds
