# Train Whistle Sound Effects

## Script Context
- George's dreams of travel and adventure
- "I'm going to see the world!" moments
- Represents George's unfulfilled wanderlust
- Likely during scenes discussing travel, trains, leaving Bedford Falls

## What to Look For
- Steam locomotive whistle (1920s-1940s era)
- Long, mournful sound
- NOT modern diesel train horns
- Classic "two-tone" or "three-chime" steam whistle

## Search Terms
- "steam train whistle"
- "vintage train whistle"
- "locomotive whistle 1940s"
- "steam engine horn"
- "classic train whistle"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "steam train whistle"
- Filter by duration (3-8 seconds ideal)

**Archive.org:**
- Historic railroad sound collections
- Public domain recordings from 1920s-1940s

**Railroad Sound Archives:**
- Many railroad museums have free sound libraries

## What to Avoid
- Modern train horns (wrong era)
- Toy train whistles (not realistic enough)
- Train crossing bells (different sound entirely)

## File Naming Convention
- `01_steam_train_long_whistle.wav`
- `02_train_distant_whistle.wav`
- `03_locomotive_close_whistle.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: MEDIUM
Frequency: 2-3 times, but emotionally important for George's character
