# Splashing Water Sound Effects

## Script Context
- **Bridge scene** - George contemplating suicide
- **Clarence jumps in** - saves George by making him save Clarence
- River/water below bridge
- Cold, winter water
- Dramatic splashes

## What to Look For
- Large splash (person jumping into water)
- Cold river sounds
- Water splashing, thrashing
- Realistic outdoor water
- Winter quality (can sound "heavy")

## Search Terms
- "person splash water"
- "jumping in water"
- "water splash large"
- "river splash"
- "swimming splashing"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "water splash" or "person water"
- Look for large, dramatic splashes

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Water section
- Swimming/splashing sounds

## Splash Variations Needed
1. **Big initial splash** - Clarence jumps in
2. **Thrashing/splashing** - George saves him
3. **Water movement** - Struggling in cold water
4. **Ambient river** - Background water sounds (optional)

## Sound Design Notes
- Should sound COLD (winter river)
- Not tropical/warm sounding
- Urgent, dramatic quality
- River current (not calm pool)

## What to Avoid
- Swimming pool sounds (too clean/echoy)
- Tropical beach waves
- Gentle, calm water
- Hot tub bubbles

## File Naming Convention
- `01_large_water_splash_jump.wav`
- `02_person_splashing_swimming.wav`
- `03_cold_river_splash.wav`
- `04_water_struggling_thrashing.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: HIGH
Frequency: 1-2 times, but CRITICAL dramatic moment
This is the turning point of the entire show!
