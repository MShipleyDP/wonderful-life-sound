## 💡 Production Tips

### Bell Motif Strategy:
- **Intimate moments:** Use small hand bells (files 01-04, 07-08)
- **Christmas atmosphere:** Use sleigh bells (file 06)
- **Dramatic revelations:** Use church bells (files 09-10)
- **Final "wings" moment:** Save the majestic church bell chime

### Charleston Party Scene:
- **Layer authentic recordings:** Mix 3-4 tracks at different volumes
- **Start with:** Charleston Foxtrot 1925 (file 05)
- **Add energy:** Party crowd cheering (file 01)
- **Background:** 1920s jazz orchestra (from folder 06)

### Bridge Scene Layering:
- **Base layer:** Calming river ambient (file 06) - loop continuously
- **Clarence splash:** Big water splash (file 01) - moment he jumps
- **George splash:** Water splashing (file 02) - moment George dives in
- **Atmosphere:** Add winter wind for cold Christmas Eve feeling
- **Struggle:** Small splashes (files 03-05) for swimming/struggling

### Bedford Falls vs. Pottersville Contrast:
- **Bedford Falls:** Use warm Italian bar (folder 20) + friendly city (folder 08)
- **Pottersville:** Use dark jazz (folder 22) + rowdy bar (folder 23)
- **Make it stark:** The contrast shows George's impact

### Time Manipulation Effect:
- **Build-up:** Slow clock ticking (folder 32, file 01)
- **Manipulation moment:** Time freeze or time stop (folder 25, files 02-03)
- **Reality shift:** Sci-fi dimensional whoosh (folder 21, file 02)
- **Aftermath:** Reverse clock ticking for disorientation (folder 25, file 05)
