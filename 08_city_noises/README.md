# General City Noises

## Script Context
- Bedford Falls downtown ambience
- Small town atmosphere
- Background for street scenes
- Builds the world of the play
- 1940s small-town America

## What to Look For
- Gentle city ambience (NOT modern traffic)
- Vintage car sounds (1940s vehicles)
- Car horns from 1940s era
- People chatting in background
- Footsteps on streets
- Gentle, nostalgic quality

## Search Terms
- "1940s city ambience"
- "vintage city sounds"
- "small town atmosphere"
- "old car horns"
- "1940s traffic"
- "vintage street sounds"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "city ambience" + filter for vintage/old
- Search: "vintage car" or "old car horn"

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Historical city recordings
- Period vehicle sounds

**Archive.org:**
- Actual 1940s city recordings
- Documentary sound archives

## Sound Elements to Layer
1. Distant traffic (old cars, lower RPM)
2. Car horns (vintage "ah-OO-gah" or simple beep)
3. People murmuring (crowd walla)
4. Footsteps on sidewalks
5. Church bells in distance (optional)

## What to Avoid
- Modern car sounds (too smooth, different engine pitch)
- Electronic sounds
- Loud/aggressive city noise (Bedford Falls is peaceful)
- Airplanes (too modern sounding)

## File Naming Convention
- `01_small_town_ambience.wav`
- `02_vintage_city_sounds.wav`
- `03_1940s_street_scene.wav`
- `04_gentle_traffic_background.wav`
- etc.
