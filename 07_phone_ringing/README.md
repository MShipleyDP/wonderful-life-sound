# Phone Ringing Sound Effects

## Script Context
- **FREQUENT USE** throughout show
- Sam Wainwright calling (multiple times)
- Building & Loan business calls
- Crisis phone calls on Christmas Eve
- Various characters calling each other

## What to Look For
- **PERIOD ACCURATE:** Rotary phone bell ring (1940s)
- NOT electronic/digital phone rings
- Classic "brring-brring" sound
- Old telephone bell
- Can vary intensity (urgent vs casual)

## Search Terms
- "old phone ringing"
- "vintage telephone ring"
- "rotary phone bell"
- "1940s phone ring"
- "antique telephone"
- "classic phone ring"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "old phone" or "rotary phone"
- Excellent variety of vintage phones

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Search: "telephone bell"
- Period-accurate recordings

**Zapsplat:**
- Good collection of vintage phones

## Ring Variations Needed
1. Normal ring (standard cadence)
2. Urgent/persistent ringing
3. Single ring (someone picks up quickly)
4. Distant phone (another room)

## Technical Notes
- 1940s phones had mechanical bells
- Classic two-tone "brring-brring"
- Different from modern trilling
- Metallic, bell-like quality

## File Naming Convention
- `01_rotary_phone_normal_ring.wav`
- `02_old_phone_urgent.wav`
- `03_vintage_phone_single_ring.wav`
- `04_phone_distant_ring.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: VERY HIGH
Frequency: 6-10+ times throughout show
Period accuracy is CRUCIAL for immersion!
