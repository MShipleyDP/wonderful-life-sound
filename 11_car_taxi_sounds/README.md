# Car / Taxi Sound Effects (Ernie's Taxi)

## Script Context
- **Ernie the taxi driver** - multiple scenes
- Ernie takes George places
- 1940s taxi cab
- Period vehicle sounds
- George's Christmas Eve journey

## What to Look For
- **1940s era vehicle sounds**
- Old car engine starting
- Vintage car driving/idling
- Car doors closing (heavier, metallic)
- Engine sounds (lower RPM than modern cars)
- Taxi horn (not modern beep)

## Sound Elements Needed
1. Car engine starting (cold start)
2. Car idling
3. Car driving away
4. Car approaching/stopping
5. Car door closing
6. Car horn (optional)

## Period Notes
- 1940s cars had:
  - Heavier, mechanical sounds
  - Lower, rougher engine tone
  - Distinct starter motor sound
  - Metal-on-metal door closing
  - Simpler, less refined engine

## What to Avoid
- Modern smooth engines
- Electronic sounds
- Power windows/locks
- Anything post-1950s
