# Breaking Glass Sound Effects

## Script Context
- **Multiple uses throughout the show** - dramatic moments, accidents
- Mr. Gower's drugstore scene (young George)
- Possibly during George's crisis moments
- General dramatic emphasis

## What to Look For
- Clean, sharp glass break (not too long)
- Single pane window breaking
- Bottle smashing (for bar scenes)
- Variety of intensities (small bottle to large window)

## Search Terms
- "glass breaking"
- "window smash"
- "bottle break"
- "glass shatter"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "glass break"
- Filter: WAV, 44.1kHz
- Popular: User "InspectorJ" has excellent glass sounds

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- https://sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk/
- Search: "glass"
- All free for non-commercial use

**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Search: "glass breaking sound effect"
- Download available MP3s

- etc.
