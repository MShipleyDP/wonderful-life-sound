# Fireworks Sound Effects

## Script Context
- Celebration scenes
- Possibly during VE/VJ Day celebrations (WWII victory)
- July 4th references
- General festive atmosphere

## What to Look For
- Variety of firework sounds: pops, bangs, whistles
- Can be single bursts or short sequences
- Realistic outdoor ambience
- 1940s era (no modern electronic fireworks)

## Search Terms
- "fireworks explosion"
- "fireworks display"
- "firework burst"
- "celebration fireworks"
- "pyrotechnics"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "fireworks"
- Look for single bursts and short sequences

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Excellent fireworks recordings
- Various intensities available

**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Search: "fireworks sound effect"

## Types Needed
1. Single large burst
2. Multiple pops/crackles
3. Whistling rocket
4. Distant fireworks (background ambience)

## File Naming Convention
- `01_fireworks_single_burst.wav`
- `02_fireworks_crackling.wav`
- `03_fireworks_whistle_bang.wav`
- `04_fireworks_distant_celebration.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: LOW-MEDIUM
Frequency: 1-2 times, celebratory moments
