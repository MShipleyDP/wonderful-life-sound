## **What This Tool Does:**

This application allows you to:
- **Listen to all sound effect options** organized by category
- **Mark your favorites** for each scene/moment
- **Rate sounds** (1-5 stars) to indicate preference
- **Add notes** about how/when to use specific sounds
- **Flag sounds for deletion** if they're not suitable

All your selections save automatically and sync in real-time.

---

## **How to Use It:**

### **Reviewing Sounds:**

1. **Browse Categories** - Click any category in the sidebar (e.g., "Bells," "Crowd," "Weather")
2. **Listen** - Click play on any sound file
3. **Mark Favorites** - Check the box on sounds you want to use
4. **Rate** - Click stars to rate (1-5) your preference level
5. **Add Notes** - Click "Add notes" to write usage instructions (e.g., "Use for Act 2, Scene 5 - George's entrance")
6. **Flag for Removal** - Click the trash icon (🗑️) if a sound isn't suitable

### **View Your Selections:**

- The sidebar shows live counts of:
  - ✓ **Checked files** - Your favorites
  - ⭐ **Rated files** - Sounds you've rated
  - 📝 **Notes** - Files with usage notes
  - 🗑️ **Flagged** - Sounds marked for deletion
