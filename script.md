\### Synopsis:



In this brand-new adaptation, ten quick-changing actors bring to life the world of Bedford Falls

and its most famous resident. The town’s citizens have relied for years on the Bailey Building

and Loan, and George Bailey has sacrificed his dreams and ambitions to keep it alive against all

odds. But on this Christmas Eve disaster strikes, and it takes a guardian angel named Clarence to

make George realize that not all is lost, and that no man is a failure who has friends. A beloved

holiday classic for the whole family.

\*\*NOTE: I imagine this as a “The 39 Steps” meets “Our Town”\*\* - the Ensemble on stage for

the whole show, costume changes are simple pieces \*\*–\*\* glasses, hats, etc. No wigs changes, no

\*\*full costume changes. Let the actors’ physicality reveal who they are. Keep is simple so that\*\*

the theatricality of the piece shines through.
