# Stars and Stripes Forever Music

## Script Context
- Patriotic celebration scenes
- Possibly VE/VJ Day (WWII victory)
- Community celebration
- American pride moment
- Triumphant, joyous atmosphere

## What to Look For
- **"The Stars and Stripes Forever"** by John Philip Sousa
- March tempo (energetic, proud)
- Full band/orchestra arrangement
- Traditional, rousing version
- PUBLIC DOMAIN recording (music is PD, recording must be too)

## About the Music
- America's national march
- Composed 1896 by John Philip Sousa
- Music is in public domain
- Need to find PD recording or license

## Search Terms
- "stars and stripes forever public domain"
- "sousa march stars and stripes"
- "stars and stripes forever recording"

**Musopen.org:**
- Free classical music recordings

## What to Look For
- Full orchestral or band version
- Clear, high-quality recording
- Traditional arrangement (not modern remix)
- Duration: 3-4 minutes (can be edited/faded)

## Usage Notes
- Can be faded in/out as needed
- Might use excerpt (most recognizable section)
- Consider volume levels vs dialogue
