# Somber Cemetery Music

## Script Context
- Cemetery scene in Pottersville
- George discovers what happened to loved ones
- Dark, mournful atmosphere
- Eerie, melancholy mood
- Emotional low point of the show

## What to Look For
- Sad, somber music
- Minimal instrumentation
- Eerie undertones
- NOT scary - just sad and lonely
- Contemplative, mournful
- Could be strings, piano, or ambient

## Search Terms
- "somber music"
- "cemetery atmosphere"
- "melancholy music"
- "sad ambient music"
- "mournful music"
- "funeral music"
- "requiem"

## Recommended Sources
**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Sad/melancholy category
- Ambient/atmospheric section

**Free Music Archive:**
- Classical/ambient categories

## Musical Styles That Work
1. **Minimal Piano** - Sparse, lonely notes
2. **String Quartet** - Slow, mournful
3. **Ambient Pads** - Dark, atmospheric
4. **Solo Cello** - Deep, sorrowful
5. **Organ** - Funeral atmosphere

## Mood
- Profound sadness
- Isolation
- Loss
- Contemplation of death/absence
- George's despair
- Eerie but not horror

## What to Avoid
- Anything uplifting
- Major keys
- Fast tempo
- Horror movie scares
- Too dramatic/melodramatic
