# Crashing Garbage Cans Sound Effects

## Script Context
- George's desperation and crisis moments
- Physical chaos during Christmas Eve crisis
- Dramatic emphasis during emotional breakdown
- Possibly when George is running through town

## What to Look For
- Metal garbage cans falling/crashing
- Multiple cans for bigger impact
- Lids rolling/clanging
- Realistic outdoor ambience
- Sharp, jarring sound for dramatic effect

## Search Terms
- "garbage cans falling"
- "trash cans crashing"
- "metal bins falling"
- "garbage can crash"
- "trash can knock over"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "garbage" or "trash can"
- Look for metal impacts

**BBC Sound Effects:**
- Search: "bin" or "dustbin"

**Zapsplat:**
- Good selection of crash sounds

## Sound Characteristics
- Loud, chaotic
- Multiple impacts (cans hitting ground, each other)
- Metal-on-metal clanging
- Reverb suggests outdoor/alley setting
- Trash contents spilling (optional detail)

## Variations Needed
1. Single can falling
2. Multiple cans crashing (bigger impact)
3. Can with lid rolling away
4. Distant garbage can crash

## File Naming Convention
- `01_garbage_cans_crash_multiple.wav`
- `02_single_trash_can_fall.wav`
- `03_metal_bins_falling.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: MEDIUM
Frequency: 1-2 times, dramatic punctuation
