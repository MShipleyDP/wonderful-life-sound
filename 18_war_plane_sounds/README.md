# War Plane Sound Effects (WWII)

## Script Context
- WWII references throughout show
- Harry Bailey in the war
- War victory celebrations
- Period: 1941-1945
- Represents global conflict affecting Bedford Falls

## What to Look For
- **WWII-era aircraft sounds**
- Propeller planes (NOT jets)
- B-17 bomber, P-51 Mustang, etc.
- Formation flying (multiple planes)
- Flyover sound

## Search Terms
- "WWII plane"
- "B-17 bomber"
- "P-51 mustang"
- "world war 2 aircraft"
- "vintage warplane"
- "propeller plane"
- "bomber flyover"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "WWII plane" or "vintage aircraft"
- Military aircraft sounds

**Archive.org:**
- Historical WWII sound recordings
- Documentary audio archives

**Aviation Museums:**
- Many have sound libraries
- Actual vintage aircraft recordings

## Aircraft Types from Era
- B-17 Flying Fortress (bomber)
- P-51 Mustang (fighter)
- B-24 Liberator
- P-47 Thunderbolt
- General propeller planes

## Sound Characteristics
- Deep propeller drone
- Multiple engines (bombers had 4 engines)
- Doppler effect as planes pass
- Distant is better than loud/close
- Can suggest scale without being overwhelming

## What to Avoid
- Jet engines (post-WWII technology)
- Modern aircraft
- Helicopter sounds
- Combat sounds (bombing, gunfire) - keep it non-violent

## File Naming Convention
- `01_wwii_bomber_flyover.wav`
- `02_vintage_warplane_distant.wav`
- `03_b17_bomber_formation.wav`
- `04_propeller_plane_passing.wav`
- etc.
