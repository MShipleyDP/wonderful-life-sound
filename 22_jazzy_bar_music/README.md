# Jazzy Bar Music (Pottersville)

## Script Context
- **Pottersville scenes** - dark alternate reality
- Seedier, harsher bar than Martini's
- Still 1940s, but darker atmosphere
- Jazz music, but with edge
- Contrast to warm Bedford Falls

## What to Look For
- 1940s jazz, but darker tone
- Blues influences
- Smoky, late-night vibe
- NOT wholesome - slightly seedy
- Nightclub/speakeasy atmosphere

## Search Terms
- "dark jazz music"
- "1940s nightclub jazz"
- "noir jazz"
- "blues jazz 1940s"
- "smoky bar jazz"
- "late night jazz"

## Recommended Sources
**YouTube Audio Library:**
- Jazz section, look for darker tones

**Free Music Archive:**
- Jazz and blues categories
- Filter by mood: dark, noir, moody

**Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod):**
- Jazz tracks
- Many film-noir appropriate pieces

## Style Notes
- Saxophone prominent
- Slower tempo than happy jazz
- Minor keys preferred
- Sultry, smoky quality
- Film noir atmosphere
- Shadows and darkness

## Mood
- Cynical, worldly
- Lost innocence
- Sophisticated but corrupt
- Contrast to wholesome Bedford Falls

## What to Avoid
- Upbeat, cheerful jazz
- Dixieland (too happy)
- Modern smooth jazz
- Anything too clean/polished

## File Naming Convention
- `01_dark_jazz_nightclub.mp3`
- `02_noir_bar_music.mp3`
- `03_smoky_jazz_blues.mp3`
- `04_pottersville_bar_jazz.mp3`
- etc.
