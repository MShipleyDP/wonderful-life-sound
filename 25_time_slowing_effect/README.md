# Time Slowing Down Effect (Joseph)

## Script Context
- Joseph (senior angel) slows down time
- Supernatural moment
- Time manipulation
- Angels controlling reality
- Otherworldly effect

## What to Look For
- Time-stretch effect
- Slowed-down sounds
- Clock ticking slowed
- Pitch-shifted down
- Whooshing/swooshing
- Sense of time stopping or slowing

## Creative Sound Design Ideas

### Option 1: Slowed Clock
- Clock ticking, gradually slowing down
- Mechanical watch winding down
- Each tick gets longer, deeper

### Option 2: Audio Time-Stretch
- Take normal sounds and stretch them
- Heartbeat slowing down
- Breathing slowed and deepened
- Reversed reverb

### Option 3: Musical/Tonal
- Descending pitch sweep
- Low, deep resonant tone
- Sub-bass rumble
- Time "pulling" sensation

### Option 4: Layered Effect
- Whoosh/swoosh sound
- + Clock slowing down
- + Deep resonance
- + Reversed elements

## Sound Characteristics
- 2-5 seconds duration
- Gradual effect (not sudden)
- Deep, low frequencies
- Sense of weight/gravity
- Supernatural but not scary
- Smooth, controlled

## Technical Approach
1. Record clock ticking
2. Gradually slow it down in audio editor
3. Add low-frequency rumble
4. Maybe add whoosh for transition
