- Young people celebrating

- Party crowd ambience (laughing, chatting)
- 1920s party atmosphere
- Glasses clinking
- Celebration cheers
- Dancing feet (Charleston)

## Sound Elements to Layer
1. Crowd walla (background chatter)
2. Laughter (young people)
3. Occasional cheers
4. Glasses clinking
5. Dancing/footsteps (optional)
6. Music in background (see folder 06 for 1920s music)

## Period Considerations
- Keep it wholesome (prohibition era)
- Not too wild or modern sounding
- Young, exuberant energy
- Optimistic, innocent feel
