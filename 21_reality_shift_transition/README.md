# Reality Shift / Transition Sound Effect

## Script Context
- **CRITICAL MOMENT:** When George enters alternate reality
- Clarence shows George world where he was never born
- Bedford Falls becomes Pottersville
- Magical, disorienting transformation
- Reality bends/shifts

## What to Look For
- Otherworldly, unsettling sound
- Sense of disorientation
- Time/space distortion
- Could be eerie, mysterious
- Builds tension

## Creative Sound Design Ideas

### Option 1: Reversed/Stretched Audio
- Reversed reverb (sounds "sucked backwards")
- Time-stretched glass harmonica
- Reversed piano notes

### Option 2: Electronic/Synthesized
- Pitch-shifted tones (descending or ascending)
- Theremin-like swoops
- Synthesizer sweep
- Ring modulation effects

### Option 3: Acoustic/Musical
- String orchestra dissonance
- Glass harmonica eerie notes
- Bowed cymbals
- Waterphone (creates eerie, sci-fi sounds)

### Option 4: Layered Natural Sounds
- Wind (processed/reversed)
- Distant thunder (pitch-shifted)
- Ice cracking (slowed down)
- All heavily processed

## Sound Characteristics
- 3-8 seconds duration
- Builds or swells
- Disorienting but not scary
- Mysterious, magical quality
- Clear beginning and end
- Not too loud/harsh
