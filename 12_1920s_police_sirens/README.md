## Siren Types from Era
1. **Hand-crank:** Mechanical, manually operated
2. **Early electric:** Motor-driven, oscillating tone
3. **Bell sirens:** Rotating bell sound

## What They Sound Like
- Warbling, mechanical tone
- "Waaah-ooh waaah-ooh"
- Lower pitch than modern sirens
- Sometimes irregular (hand-cranked)
- Distinct from European sirens

## What to Avoid
- Modern electronic "wail" or "yelp" sirens
- European "nee-naw" sirens
- Air raid sirens (too dramatic)
- Ambulance sirens (different pitch)
