# Bell Sound Effects (Angel Wings)

## Script Context
- **KEY MOTIF** - "Every time a bell rings, an angel gets his wings"
- Opening scene with angels
- Throughout the show when angels appear
- **CRITICAL:** Final scene - Zuzu's bell rings, Clarence gets his wings
- May be used as transition sound between scenes

## What to Look For
- Clear, bright, cheerful bell
- Small hand bell (like a service bell or dinner bell)
- NOT church bells (too large/deep)
- Pleasant, magical quality
- Clean ring with natural decay

## Search Terms
- "small bell ring"
- "hand bell"
- "dinner bell"
- "service bell"
- "desk bell"
- "silver bell"

## Recommended Sources
**Freesound.org:**
- Search: "small bell" or "hand bell"
- Look for single, clear rings

**Free Stock Music:**
- https://www.freestockmusic.com/
- Search sound effects section

**Zapsplat:**
- https://www.zapsplat.com/
- Free with attribution

## Specific Recommendations
Look for bells with:
- 1-3 second duration
- Bright, clear tone
- Natural reverb/decay
- No background noise

## File Naming Convention
- `01_bell_bright_clear.wav`
- `02_bell_soft_magical.wav`
- `03_bell_hand_ring.wav`
- etc.

## Priority: VERY HIGH
Frequency: 5-8 times throughout show
This is one of the most important sound effects!
